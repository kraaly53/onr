One of the co-founders of Overnight Radio Inc., and a graduate from Fanshawe College in both the Recording Industry/Digital Applications and the Music Industry Arts programs, Darren is of the technical set. It was his recording studio that first brought him and Jaysen together. With Jaysen's idea and Darren's musical production, technical talents and commercial production experience at Kool FM in Waterloo, Ontario, the realization of the overnight radio production house was born.
