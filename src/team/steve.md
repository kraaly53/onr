Steve got his start in Armed Forces Radio in 1970. A short-lived college career pointed the way to his first love: Radio. His hometown of Toledo, Ohio wasn't big enough so he headed west to Los Angeles in 1978. He quickly landed the afternoon drive post at top-rated KJOI and then became a national instructor for the Columbia School of Broadcasting.
