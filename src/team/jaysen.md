One of the co-founders of Overnight Radio Inc... A graduate of Seneca College in Creative Advertising and Radio & Television Broadcasting. He later worked in southern Ontario stations such as KOOL FM, CKNX, EZ-ROCK, and KICX FM as a writer, Creative Director, and Production Director. With the growing realization of an industry need, he decided to seek out the right business partner and start the company known as ONR.
